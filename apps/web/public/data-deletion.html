Delete your data

Bid Wit Me (bid-wit-me.pages.dev)

Delete your account in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Bid Wit Me and sign in.
  2. Tap your name in the top-left corner of the start screen to open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This happens immediately and can't be undone. It permanently deletes your username, every linked sign-in (Google, Discord, X, Facebook), your passkeys, your sessions and your saved progress.

If you signed in with Facebook

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. Find Bid Wit Me and tap Remove, then choose to send a deletion request.

Facebook tells us automatically. We delete your Facebook login and the name we received from Facebook. If Facebook was your only way to sign in, the whole account is deleted. You'll get a confirmation code; open this page with that code to check its status.

Guest data on your device

Settings, badges, series and game saves for guests live only in your browser. To remove them, clear this site's data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings.

Feedback you sent

To have feedback messages or screenshots removed, send a request with Send feedback in the game (the ⚙ More options menu) and tell us roughly when you sent it.
