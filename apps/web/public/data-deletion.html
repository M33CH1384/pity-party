Pity Party (pity-party.pages.dev)

Delete your account in the game

  1. Open Pity Party and sign in with your passkey.
  2. Tap your name at the top of the start screen to open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type your username to confirm, and tap Delete forever.

This happens immediately and can't be undone. You're signed out on this device and every session ends. It permanently deletes:

  • your username, profile picture, tagline, links and region, your passkeys and any linked sign-ins;
  • your coins (balance and history), daily bonus streak, badges, challenges, unlocks and saved progress;
  • your friends and friend requests, blocks, mutes, direct messages, pending invites, reports you sent, privacy settings, online status and "last seen";
  • your push notification subscriptions and settings.

Games you played with other people stay in their records, but your seat is anonymised ("deleted") so it no longer points to you. Lines you posted in the public chat roll off as new lines arrive (the last ~200 are kept); ask us if you want one removed sooner.

If you used Facebook sign-in

Facebook sign-in isn't switched on right now. If it is offered later and you used it, you can also remove Pity Party under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites on Facebook and send a deletion request; Facebook tells us automatically, and you can open this page with the confirmation code you receive to check its status.

Guest data on your device

Settings, coins, badges and game saves for guests live only in your browser. To remove them, clear this site's data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings.

Feedback you sent

To have feedback messages or screenshots removed, send a request with Send feedback in the game (the ⚙ More options menu) and tell us roughly when you sent it.
