Privacy Policy

Pity Party (pity-party.pages.dev) · Effective October 26, 2026

Pity Party is a free card game (Pitty Pat, Tonk and more) in beta, run by Demetrius Robinson. This page explains what the game keeps, why, and how to delete it. Short version: you can play without an account, the game keeps only what it needs to work, coins are play money with no cash value, and we never sell your data or show ads.

Playing as a guest (no account)

  • On your device: your settings, theme, the table you're in, your coins and badges and your display name are kept in your browser's local storage. They don't leave your device unless you sign in and choose to save your progress.
  • Online tables: to run a table, the server keeps the game state, the display names at the table, the table chat and a secret seat key per player so you can rejoin. A table is deleted automatically after 24 hours with nobody connected.
  • Lobby and social chat: guests can read and post in the public chat under their display name. The server keeps the most recent ~200 lines.
  • Video and voice: camera and microphone are off until you turn them on. Audio and video go directly between players' devices (WebRTC), or through an encrypted relay if a direct connection isn't possible. We don't record or store them.
  • Music links: if a host shares a Spotify or Apple Music link and you tap it, it opens in that app under that company's own privacy policy.

If you create an account (optional)

Accounts use a passkey. (Sign-in with Google, Discord, X or Facebook may be offered later; if it is, we ask only for basic profile access and never receive your password, email address or contacts, and never post for you.) With an account we keep:

  • Your username and when the account was created; the passkey's public key and ID (your fingerprint or face data never leaves your device).
  • Sessions: a sign-in cookie (HttpOnly, Secure). We store only a one-way hash of it; sessions expire after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • Profile extras you choose to add: a profile picture, a tagline, social links and a self-declared region.
  • Game records: for online games, the mode, the seats (names and account IDs), finishing places and coin results, used for your profile stats.
  • Coins and progress: your coin balance and coin history, daily bonus streak, badges, challenges and unlocks. Coins are play money only: they can't be bought, sold, cashed out or exchanged for anything of value.
  • Social: friends and friend requests, blocks and mutes, direct messages, table invites (offline invites expire after 10 minutes), reports you send, your privacy settings (appear offline, friends-only invites), and what you're doing right now (online, in the lobby, at a table) plus when you were last online, shown only to your friends and never while you appear offline.
  • Push notifications (only if you turn them on): your browser's push address and keys, which notification types you want, and when we last sent each one (to avoid repeats).

Feedback

If you send feedback from the game, we keep your message, any screenshots you attach, the optional name and email you type, basic technical context (game screen, device and browser type), and a one-way hash of your IP address (used only to limit spam). Feedback may be emailed to the developer through Resend.

Security and abuse limits

To stop spam, the server counts requests per network using a one-way hash of your IP address. These counts are short-lived and aren't linked to your account.

Who processes data

  • Cloudflare hosts the game, the server and the database.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple) delivers notifications if you turn them on.
  • Resend delivers feedback emails to the developer.

There are no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no selling or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

In the game, tap your name at the top of the start screen to open Account, then Delete account and type your username to confirm. This permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it right away. In shared game records your seat is anonymised so the other players' results stay correct. Guest data on your device can be cleared by clearing this site's data in your browser. See Delete your data for details.

Children

Pity Party isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions or requests: use Send feedback in the game (the ⚙ More options menu).
