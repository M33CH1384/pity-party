Privacy Policy

Bid Wit Me (bid-wit-me.pages.dev) · Effective October 11, 2026

Bid Wit Me is a free Spades card game run by Demetrius Robinson. This page explains what the game collects, why, and how to delete it. Short version: you can play without an account, we only keep what the game needs to work, and we never sell your data or show ads.

Playing as a guest (no account)

  • On your device: your settings, the game you're in, badges, rematch series, team name and display name are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose "Save my progress" after signing in.
  • Online tables: to run a table, our server keeps the table's game state, the display names at the table, chat sent at the table, and a secret seat key per player so you can rejoin. A table is deleted automatically after 24 hours with nobody connected.
  • Video and voice: camera and microphone are off until you turn them on. Audio and video go directly between players' devices (WebRTC). If a direct connection isn't possible, they may pass through a relay, encrypted. We don't record or store them.
  • Music links: if a host shares a Spotify or Apple Music link and you tap it, it opens in that app under that company's own privacy policy.

If you create an account (optional)

You can sign in with Google, Discord, X, Facebook or a passkey. We keep:

  • Your username (chosen by you) and when the account was created.
  • For each linked sign-in: the provider's user ID for you and your display name from that provider. We ask only for basic profile access (Google: openid profile; Discord: identify; X: users.read tweet.read; Facebook: public_profile). We do not receive your email address, password, friends or contacts, and we never post anything for you.
  • For passkeys: the passkey's public key and ID. Your fingerprint or face data stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Sessions: a sign-in cookie (HttpOnly, secure). We store only a one-way hash of it. Sessions expire after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • Progress you choose to save: badges, your current rematch series and your team name.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the game, we keep your message, any screenshots you attach, the optional name and email you type, basic technical context (game screen, device and browser type), and a one-way hash of your IP address (used only to limit spam). Feedback may be emailed to the developer through Resend.

Security and abuse limits

To stop spam, the server counts requests per network using a one-way hash of your IP address. These counts live in memory for minutes and are not stored.

Who processes data

  • Cloudflare hosts the game, the server and the database.
  • Resend delivers feedback emails to the developer.
  • Google, Discord, X and Meta (Facebook) handle the sign-in step when you choose them, under their own privacy policies.

There are no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no selling or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

In the game, tap your name in the top-left corner of the start screen, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, linked sign-ins, passkeys, sessions and saved progress right away. Guest data on your device can be cleared by clearing this site's data in your browser. See Delete your data for all options, including removing the app from Facebook.

Children

Bid Wit Me isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions or requests: use Send feedback in the game (the ⚙ More options menu).
